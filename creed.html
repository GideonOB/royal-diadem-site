The Royal Unveiling

A declaration of identity, direction, and daily conduct for Royal Diadem women.

Read the Creed as Text

I am a Royal Diadem.

I am a Crown of glory in the hand of the LORD.

I am a precious jewel in the hand of my Creator.

My value is not determined by society,
trends, or the opinions of others.

My worth is determined solely by my Manufacturer—
God Almighty.

I refuse to be carried away by fleeting trends.

I am rooted in the Word.

I walk with confidence, not arrogance.

I speak with authority, yet I serve with humility.

I am beautiful, not just in appearance but in spirit
and in character.

When I speak, I speak wisdom.

I possess the fruits of the Spirit, skills, and abilities
to change my world.

I know who I am.

I am a Child of the King,
God’s own purchased special daughter,
A Royal Diadem.
